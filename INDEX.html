Welcome to North Star Bakery

We are a small neighborhood bakery on Linden Avenue. Everything in the case is mixed, shaped, and baked by hand each morning, and we love knowing our customers by name. Stop in on your way to work, bring the kids on Saturday, or let us bake for your next gathering.

What We're Known For

  • Breads Slow-fermented sourdough and sandwich loaves, baked fresh every morning.
  • Pastries Butter croissants, cinnamon rolls, cookies, and seasonal fruit danishes.
  • Cakes Made-to-order for birthdays, weddings, and community events.
  • Fast Counter Service Friendly help that gets commuters out the door quickly.
